Menu Comparison Study

This is the ab_experiment project for Bride of Frankensystem. It's a demonstration, not a real research study — no data is being collected for any purpose and the cover story is fictional.

The text on this page is placeholder consent text; in a real BOFS project it would be replaced with ethics-approved language describing the actual study, risks, data handling, and the right to withdraw.

This is a short study about two ways of choosing commands in software interfaces. You'll be assigned to one of two menu styles and complete a small set of practice trials with that interface. The whole study takes a few minutes.
