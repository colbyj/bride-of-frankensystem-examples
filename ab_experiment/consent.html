A/B Experiment Example

This is the ab_experiment project for Bride of Frankensystem. It's a demonstration, not a real research study. Any collected data will be discarded, and any cover story is fictional.

This example demonstrates how to run a between-subjects A/B study in Bride of Frankensystem, including:

  • Two conditions (Linear Menu vs. Marking Menu) balanced across participants by BOFS's greedy assigner.
  • A conditional_routing block that shows each participant only their condition's instructions and task pages.
  • A shared demographics questionnaire collected before condition assignment branches.
  • A custom blueprint that implements the menu-selection task and records trial data.
