Simple BOF Project Example

This is the minimal_example project for Bride of Frankensystem. It's a demonstration, not a real research study — no data is being collected for any purpose and the cover story is fictional.

The text on this page is placeholder consent text; in a real BOFS project it would be replaced with ethics-approved language describing the actual study, risks, data handling, and the right to withdraw.

This is a minimal example project that demonstrates the basics of Bride of Frankensystem: a questionnaire with a variety of question types, a questionnaire that performs calculations when exporting data, and a questionnaire that is shown more than once (for example, to collect pre-task and post-task measurements).
