Redirecting Based on Source Example

This is the redirect_by_source project for Bride of Frankensystem. It's a demonstration, not a real research study. Any collected data will be discarded, and any cover story is fictional.

This example demonstrates the basics of Bride of Frankensystem, including:

  • A questionnaire with a variety of question types.
  • A questionnaire that performs calculations on participant responses when exporting data.
  • Showing the same questionnaire more than once (for example, to collect pre-task and post-task measurements).

The admin panel is accessible at /admin. The password is example.
