Learning Phase — Marking Menu

You've been assigned to the Marking Menu condition. Commands are arranged radially around a central handle. To pick one, you press and hold on the handle, drag toward the wedge labeled with the command you want, and release.

On the next page you'll see a centred Hold button surrounded (once you press it) by six labeled wedges. Each trial:

  1. A prompt at the top of the screen names a command (e.g., "Select Save").
  2. Press and hold the mouse on Hold. The six wedges appear.
  3. While still holding, drag the cursor onto the wedge labeled with the prompted command.
  4. Release to make your selection. (If you release outside any wedge, nothing happens — just press and try again.)

You'll do 12 practice trials in total. After each one you'll see whether you got it right. Try to remember the direction (clock position) of each command — tomorrow you'll be tested on it.
