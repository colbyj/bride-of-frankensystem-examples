Stroop Task

On each trial you will see a colour word (for example, the word RED) shown in coloured ink. Your job is to identify the colour of the ink, ignoring the word itself.

Press the key that matches the ink colour:

  • R — red
  • G — green
  • B — blue
  • Y — yellow

Respond as quickly and accurately as you can. There are 24 trials and the task takes about two minutes. Click next below when you are ready to begin.
