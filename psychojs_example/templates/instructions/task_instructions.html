Stroop Task

On each trial you will see a colour word (for example, the word RED) shown in coloured letters. Your job is to identify the colour of the letters, ignoring the word itself.

Use the arrow keys to respond:

  • ← Left arrow — red letters
  • ↓ Down arrow — green letters
  • → Right arrow — blue letters

Respond as quickly and accurately as you can. There are 45 trials and the task takes about three minutes. The task page will load with a black background; the experiment begins automatically. Press Esc at any time to abort. Click next below when you are ready to begin.
