Branching Example

This is the branching_example project for Bride of Frankensystem. It's a demonstration, not a real research study — no data is being collected for any purpose and the cover story is fictional.

The text on this page is placeholder consent text; in a real BOFS project it would be replaced with ethics-approved language describing the actual study, risks, data handling, and the right to withdraw.

The example walks through three kinds of branching: questions on a single page that show or hide based on what you've answered so far, a routing block that sends participants down different sets of pages based on their screening answer, and a single optional page shown based on an independent screening question.
