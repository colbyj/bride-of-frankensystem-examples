Branching Example

This is the branching_example project for Bride of Frankensystem. It's a demonstration, not a real research study. Any collected data will be discarded, and any cover story is fictional.

This example demonstrates the three kinds of branching available in Bride of Frankensystem, including:

  • Question-level show_if: a follow-up question that appears only when a particular answer is selected.
  • A conditional_routing block driven by a participant's own answer (rather than an assigned condition), sending them down different sets of pages.
  • Page-level show_if: a single optional page shown only when an independent screening question matches.
